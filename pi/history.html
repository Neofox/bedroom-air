How a drop is identified

A drop of at least 150 ppm over 5–30 minutes with several falling readings counts as a possible airing. A door opening or someone leaving can produce a similar pattern. Gaps split the record.

Cooling compares the temperature at the start with its lowest reading during the drop. After a cooling of at least 0.3 °C, the Pi looks for three consecutive readings back within 0.2 °C of the starting temperature or warmer, for up to two hours.
